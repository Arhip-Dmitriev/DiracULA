DIRACULA DOCUMENTATION
======================

The ZX View: Language States as Diagrams

Applies to: DiracULA 0.1.x
Modules: diracula.zxview, diracula.zxsym, diracula.zx.draw, diracula.zx.listing


1. PURPOSE
----------

Any state or density matrix of the .ula language can be shown as a ZXW diagram (views --zx,
--zxunicode, --zxascii, --both and --raw; SHELL.txt, section 3.2). The diagram is built from
the structure of the path sum, not from its amplitudes, so a state of symbolic size has a
diagram of fixed size: the same graph for every value of the size symbols, with bang boxes
carrying the symbolic counts.


2. TRANSLATION OF ONE TERM (diracula.zxsym)
--------------------------------------------------

A path-sum term is an amplitude times a sum over bound indices of register values. It is
translated as follows.

  - Registers of one shape (w digits of base d) form a family. The digits of a family share
    bang boxes of multiplicity w, split where a single digit is singled out (bit(x, k), a
    literal digit, or bitdot(x, c)).
  - A summation index ranging over a whole register is a digit index: one Z spider per digit
    inside the boxes.
  - Phases acting digit by digit (digit sums dsum, digit products ddot, a digit's own value)
    become phases on those spiders and Fourier boxes between them.
  - A qubit ket whose value is an xor of digits is an X spider; a literal digit is an X spider.
  - Any other index is a wide index: one Z spider on a wire of its whole range.
  - Any other amplitude factor is a gadget: the wires of the indices it reads are bound into
    one wire ending in a Z effect whose phase rule is the factor's turns (complex turns for a
    weight that is not a phase).
  - Any other ket value v on a register of L levels is an output spider u read through
    [u = v] = (1/L) sum_t omega^{t (u - v)}: a Fourier box between u and t, and a gadget on t
    and the indices of v.

Two terms without summation indices whose amplitudes differ by a phase rho, a0 |v0> + a0 rho
|v1>, become one term sum_{c=0}^{1} a0 rho^c |v0 + c (v1 - v0)>. The GHZ state
(|0>^@n + |1>^@n)/sqrt(2) is then one Z spider copied into the n digits of the register.
Several other terms are folded into one with a selector index s: the factors of term i read
s == i, and each register's value becomes a function of s and the term indices.

An amplitude outside the scalar and phase grammar, such as cos(t) of a real symbol t, has no
ZX form; the view prints "no ZX form (reason)".


3. SUMS OF TERMS AND LITERAL VECTORS (diracula.zxview)
-------------------------------------------------------------

Several terms of a concrete state are summed exactly by a selector: a W node fans
|1, payload> out to one copy per term, each term being a block of digit spiders. A sum without
that form is folded into one term as in section 2.

A one-register ket given by literal amplitudes c_k is drawn from its amplitude vector: with s
the first index such that c_s != 0, set u_k = c_{(k+s) mod D} / c_s. A W node read backwards
adds the components of the Z effects on its outputs,

    u = W^T (z_1 (x) ... (x) z_m),   z_j = sum_k e^{i alpha_j[k]} |k>,
    u_k = sum_j e^{i alpha_j[k]},

with m = 0 for u = |0>, m = 1 when every |u_k| = 1, and otherwise m even, m >= max |u_k|, the
phases coming in pairs arg u_k +- acos(|u_k| / m).

A density matrix is drawn from its vectorisation: the ket registers, then the bra registers.

A state with no structural translation raises ZXViewError; the shell then reports that the
state has no ZX diagram.


4. DRAWING (diracula.zx.draw)
-----------------------------

The layout places nodes in columns by their distance from the outputs, outputs rightmost,
splits wires that skip columns with dummy points, and orders each column by barycentre sweeps
to reduce crossings. A diagram of more than 150 nodes is summarised: each summed term becomes
one box, and chains of splitters or binders one node.

Three renderers share the layout:

  matplot   a matplotlib figure (Z spiders green, X spiders red, Fourier boxes yellow,
            bang boxes dashed with their multiplicity, the scalar above), shown inline in the
            terminal or saved by --images
  unicode   a character grid; Z and X spiders as Ⓩ and Ⓧ, crossings as ┼
  ascii     the same in ASCII, spiders as (Z ..) and (X ..), crossings as +

Long phase rules are listed in a legend. Boundary wires are labelled out i, or ket i and bra i
for a density matrix. With :listing the graph listing (diracula.zx.listing.render_graph) is
printed under each drawing; the raw view prints only the listing, nothing summarised.
