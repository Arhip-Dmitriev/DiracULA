==========================

abstracts, surveys and search results. The SWTA, CoqQ and Qbricks paper bodies were not read.

2. CANDIDATE NOVELTY, STRONGEST FIRST
-------------------------------------

  N1  Symbolic d with computational-basis arithmetic (x mod d, ω^(xy/d), registers of d^n
      levels) in an automated tool. No such tool was found. DiracDec is dimension-independent
      only for abstract Hilbert spaces and cannot express the mod-d and modular-phase
      arithmetic of a qudit QFT or of Deutsch-Jozsa with f(x) = x mod d.
  N2  Forward computation of closed forms. Every automated verifier above checks a
      user-supplied specification or equation; DiracULA produces the answer.
  N3  Automatic per-result Lean certificates through a verified normaliser. DiracDec and
      D-Hammer list certificates as future work; the foundational tools need manual proofs.
      Holds only within the certified fragment (docs/LEAN.txt, section 10; ALEXANDER_SPEC.txt).
  N4  Symbolic Born distributions derived automatically for all n and d. Plausibly new;
      weaker, since the others handle probabilities through manual proofs.


3. NOT NOVEL ON THEIR OWN
-------------------------

  - Path sums (Amy; Qbricks).
  - Symbolic n (Qbricks, SWTA, CoqQ, SQIR, Isabelle Marries Dirac).
  - A Dirac-notation language or automated Dirac-notation reasoning (DiracDec, D-Hammer).

The novelty is the combination N1-N3.


4. TO VERIFY BEFORE SUBMISSION
------------------------------

  - SWTA paper body: qudit support and whether it outputs states. The closest work on
    "fully automatic and parametric".
  - D-Hammer index layer: whether its index types can carry dimension arithmetic.
  - CoqQ and Qbricks: qudit or symbolic-dimension support.
